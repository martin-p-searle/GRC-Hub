# GDPR Compliance Resources (Fictional)

## Overview
A self-directed training project applying the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) to a fictional organisation that handles EU personal data. All organisations, people and data are invented, and the material is for educational and portfolio purposes only.

## Contents
- [GDPR Gap Analysis](GDPR_Gap_Analysis.md): identifies gaps between current practice and GDPR requirements, such as data subject rights and consent management, with proposed remediation steps
- [DSAR Process](DSAR_Process.md): procedure for handling requests from individuals to access, rectify or delete their personal data
- [Data Processing Agreements](Data_Processing_Agreements.md): Article 28 agreement template for third-party processors
- Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) template, as required by Article 35
- Privacy policies covering data processing, consent and breach notification
- Staff training materials on handling personal data and responding to breaches

## Notes
- This is a **fictional** project for educational purposes.
- Nothing here is legal advice.
